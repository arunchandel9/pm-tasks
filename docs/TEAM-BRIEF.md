# Team brief (post in Task Hub Feed)

Hi all. From today, every client ask goes through **Task Hub**. It turns what a client asks into a Pulp card and a row in PM Overview, and keeps a record you can ask questions of.

**Send asks here, nothing else changes:**
- 📥 **Task Hub Drop** space: share from your phone (WhatsApp text, voice note, screenshot with a typed line).
- On a computer: DM **Task Hub** in Chat, or forward the mail to **taskhub@mangoeyesagency.com**.
- Start with the client when the message does not say it: `HOH: the booking form is broken`.

**Three rules:**
1. The feed line in this space is your receipt. Nothing in 2 minutes → send again.
2. If the hub asks "Which client?", answer in that thread.
3. PMs: a task arrives as a **proposal card** in the feed thread, addressed to you, with Department, Assign to, Priority and Due already filled in. Tap **Create card** and it is in To Do, assigned, row written. **Remind me instead** or **No card** are the other two taps. (Before 22 Sep: new cards waited in **Staging** on the department board. Dragging the card out is the approval; the sheet row appears by itself, yellow until you check it.

Guide, one page per role: `docs/GUIDE.md` (link). Questions to me.


## What changed on 22 September (post this as a second message)

Task Hub now makes fewer cards, and none without you.

- Every message becomes one of five things: a task, a reminder, an idea, a rule for a client, or a note. Only a task can become a card.
- A task comes to you as a card in the feed thread, with Department, Assign to, Priority and Due filled in. Check, change if needed, tap **Create card**. It lands in To Do on the board, assigned, sheet row written. **Remind me instead** sets a reminder for you. **No card** makes nothing. No more Staging, no more dragging.
- Say "remind me tomorrow" or "chase this on Friday" in the Drop and the hub reminds you, @mentioned, until you press Done.
- Ideas come back every Monday at 10:00 for a decision: Make it a task, or Not now.
- The daily brief now comes at 10:00, lists only what is waiting on a named person, and does not post when nothing is.
